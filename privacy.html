Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 26, 2026

Local SlowMo Frame is designed to process videos locally on the user's device. We do not operate a cloud upload workflow for app editing features, and we do not collect or store users' video content on our servers.

Information We Access

The app may request access to the Photo Library for the following purposes:

  • Importing videos selected by the user
  • Saving edited videos back to the user's Photo Library

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal data or video content through the app's core editing flow.

Accounts and Tracking

Local SlowMo Frame does not require account creation. The app does not use third-party advertising SDKs or user tracking for the core product experience.
